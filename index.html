The ten platycosms

Closed flat 3-manifolds, each drawn as one fundamental cell. Tubes lie on its edges: tubes of the same colour are the same edge of the space, and the arrowheads point the same way along it. Dots are vertices; same colour means same point. The crab is a figure that lets you see how the copies are related.
