**How to read the pictures.** Each space is drawn as one cell, a box whose faces are glued in pairs: walk out through one face and you walk in through its partner. Tubes of the same colour are the same edge of the space, and the arrowheads show which way round they are glued. Balls of the same colour are the same point. The crab shows how you arrive: shifted, turned, or mirrored (watch the coral claw and the R on its back).
