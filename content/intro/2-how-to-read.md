**How to read the pictures:** In the outside view, each space is drawn as a box with faces glued in pairs: leave through one face and you enter through its partner. Tubes of the same colour are the same edge, with arrows showing how they're glued together; balls of the same colour are the same point. In the inside view, you see how the world would look if you lived inside the platycosm; each crab is the same, seen from different angles as light loops around the space.
