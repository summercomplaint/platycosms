**How to read the pictures:** In the outside view, each space is drawn as a box with faces glued in pairs: walk out through one face and you walk in through its partner. Tubes of the same colour are the same edge, with arrows showing how they're glued together. Balls of the same colour are the same point. In the inside view, you see how the world would look if you lived inside the platycosm; each crab is the same, seen from different angles as light loops around the space.
