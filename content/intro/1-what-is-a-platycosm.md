A **platycosm** ("flat world") is a possible shape for a finite universe with locally Euclidean geometry: every small region looks exactly like ordinary space, but travelling far enough in a straight line can leave you back where you started. Up to the choice of side lengths there are exactly ten of them (for why, see Appendix 1 in the paper, or [this diagram](figure:proof)). Six are [orientable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orientability); in the other four, a round trip can bring you back to where you started as your own mirror image.
