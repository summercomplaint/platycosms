A **platycosm** is a possible shape for a finite universe that is flat: every small region looks exactly like ordinary space, but go far enough in a straight line and you come back to where you started. Up to the choice of sizes there are exactly ten of them. Six are [orientable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orientability); in the other four, a round trip can bring you back as your own mirror image. The names are Conway and Rossetti's.
