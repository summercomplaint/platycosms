As in the [first amphicosm](#a1p), two opposite faces are glued after a mirror flip: here the bottom to the top, flipped left to right. The difference is that the front face is glued to the back in two halves, each shifted half way left or right, so it is not a product with a circle. 

Also like the [first amphicosm](#a1p), it is [non-orientable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orientability).
