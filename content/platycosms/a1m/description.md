As in the [first amphicosm](#a1p), the left face is glued to the right after a mirror flip. The difference is that the front face is glued to the back in two halves, each shifted half way up or down, so it is not a product with a circle. The line round the middle of the box marks the halves.

Also like the [first amphicosm](#a1p), it is [non-orientable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orientability).
