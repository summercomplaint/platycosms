Also known as the [3-torus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3-torus).

Each face of the cube is glued straight across to the opposite face, like a video game screen that wraps around, but in all three directions. Leave through the right face and you come back in through the left, unchanged.

Every slice is a torus, and going up through the cell brings it back exactly as it was: the torus times a circle, the [mapping torus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mapping_torus) of doing nothing.
