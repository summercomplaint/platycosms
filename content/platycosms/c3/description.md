The cell is a hexagonal prism. Opposite side faces are glued straight across; the top is glued to the bottom with a 120° twist.

Only half, third, quarter and sixth turns carry a flat torus onto itself (the [crystallographic restriction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crystallographic_restriction_theorem)), so there are exactly five spaces built like this: [c1](#c1), [c2](#c2), c3, [c4](#c4) and [c6](#c6). The turn can go either way, and the two choices are mirror images of each other.
