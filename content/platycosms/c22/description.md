Also known as the [Hantzsche–Wendt manifold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hantzsche–Wendt_manifold).

The odd one out. The left and right faces are glued straight across, but the other two pairs are glued by a shift combined with a 90° rotation. 

It is the one platycosm that is not a [mapping torus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mapping_torus): its first homology group is finite, so there is no way to map it onto a circle and look at the slices.
