The odd one out. The left and right faces are glued straight across, but the other two pairs are glued by half-turn screws about lines in two perpendicular directions. The top and bottom are each glued in two halves, each half by its own screw. The line round the middle of the box marks the halves, and makes the box read as two cubes side by side.

It is the one platycosm that is not a [mapping torus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mapping_torus): its first homology group is finite, so there is no way to map it onto a circle and look at the slices.
