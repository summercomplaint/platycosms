The side faces are glued straight across, as in the torocosm, but the top is glued to the bottom after a half turn. A crab that climbs out through the top comes back in through the bottom facing the other way.

Every horizontal slice of the cell, with its sides glued, is a flat [torus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torus). Moving up through the cell sweeps that torus once around a circle, and when it gets back it has been given a half turn. So the dicosm is the [mapping torus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mapping_torus) of a half turn of the torus.
