Opposite side faces are glued straight across; the top is glued to the bottom after a quarter turn.

Every horizontal slice of the cell, with its sides glued, is a flat [torus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torus). Moving up through the cell sweeps that torus once around a circle, and it comes back turned by a quarter. So the tetracosm is the [mapping torus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mapping_torus) of a quarter turn. Turning the other way gives its mirror image.
